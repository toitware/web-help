Most C3 boards I know don't use the UART for logging, though.

Depends. I think one can use the RMT for serial simulation, but more common is to have one uart that switches between different pins depending on who to talk to.
If the devices are poll based that works fine. If they are emitting data on their own then you probably want to try the RMT.
